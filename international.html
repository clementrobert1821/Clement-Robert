Sustainability and Civic Engagement

← Back to Contents

I've already taken part in a few initiatives related to civic engagement and sustainability.

These experiences have helped me become more aware of today's social and environmental challenges, while developing teamwork and organizational skills. Below are some examples of the projects I've contributed to.

Futur Proche Festival – 2025 Edition

As part of the 2025 Futur Proche Festival, I took part in the workshop “Let’s Design Our Low-Carbon Lives” at ENAC, where we discussed different ways to reduce our individual carbon emissions. This collaborative activity allowed me to better understand the importance of sustainable lifestyles and to reflect on the collective challenges of the ecological transition.

In the afternoon, I attended the conference “Aviation, Climate and Energy Transition: Which Scenarios ?” at ISAE-SUPAERO. This event helped me gain a clearer view of the environmental issues facing the aeronautical industry and the complexity of building a more sustainable future for this sector.

Futur Proche Festival – 2026 Edition

Building on my previous experience, I participated in the 2026 edition of the Futur Proche Festival. I attended a conference titled “Penser les effets de l’IA générative” (Thinking about the effects of generative AI), which provided valuable insights into the societal, economic, and environmental impacts of emerging artificial intelligence technologies.

Additionally, I took part in the workshop “Quelle éthique pour l’ingénieur.e ?” (What ethics for the engineer?). This collaborative session encouraged profound reflection on the moral responsibilities and ethical decision-making required in our future engineering careers when designing systems that shape tomorrow's world.

Fresque du Climat

To raise awareness about climate issues, I took part in the “Fresque du Climat” workshop. This collaborative and educational activity allowed me to better understand the chain of causes and effects behind climate change.

Working as a team made it easier to discuss ideas and share perspectives on how our lifestyles impact the planet. It was both insightful and motivating, reinforcing my willingness to contribute to more sustainable practices in my future career as an engineer.

Play "The trial of King Kong"

I attended the play “Le procès de King Kong”, which explores the issue of gender-based and sexual violence. The performance offered a striking and thought-provoking perspective on how power, responsibility, and social awareness intertwine. It made me reflect on the importance of questioning biases and recognizing the impact of individual and collective actions in society.

CSR commitment

As part of the Civic Engagement program at ENSEEIHT, I am co-developing Generations Connect, a project aimed at combating the social isolation facing over 2 million seniors in France.

Our team plans to establish a partnership with a local nursing home to organize weekly visits focused on recreational activities and digital inclusion, addressing UNESCO Sustainable Development Goals.

Acting as the Communications Manager, my role is to design the impact measurement strategy and plan stakeholder communication, allowing me to develop strong communication and management skills in a project management context.
