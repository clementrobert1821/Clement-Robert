Academic Exchange Project

As part of my engineering curriculum at INP ENSEEIHT, developing an international profile is a key objective. The aeronautical industry—my targeted career field—is inherently global, requiring engineers to collaborate effectively across borders and cultures.

To achieve this, I will be undertaking an academic exchange semester at the prestigious University of Sydney in Australia, starting in early 2027. This confirmed placement represents a unique opportunity to experience a different educational approach, discover a new culture, and deepen my engineering knowledge in a highly stimulating, English-speaking environment.

Language Proficiency

To validate my English proficiency for this international exchange, I took the IELTS Academic exam in October 2026. I achieved an overall band score of 7.5 / 9.0, which certifies a C1 Advanced level on the CEFR scale. This result demonstrates my ability to work, study, and communicate efficiently in demanding academic and professional international settings.
