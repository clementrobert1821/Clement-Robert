Academic Exchange Project

As part of my engineering curriculum at INP ENSEEIHT, developing an international profile is a key objective. The aeronautical industry—my targeted career field—is inherently global, requiring engineers to collaborate effectively across borders and cultures.

To achieve this, I am actively preparing an application for an academic exchange semester abroad, planned for early 2027. My primary goal is to study at the prestigious University of Sydney in Australia. This destination represents a unique opportunity to experience a different educational approach, discover a new culture, and deepen my engineering knowledge in a highly stimulating, English-speaking environment.

Language Proficiency

To satisfy the rigorous international academic exchange requirements and validate my English proficiency, I took the IELTS Academic exam in October 2026. This thorough preparation has significantly strengthened my ability to work, study, and communicate efficiently in an international academic and professional setting.
