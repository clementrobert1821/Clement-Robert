Recreational Football

I regularly play recreational football with friends on weekends, which helps me stay active and maintain a good balance alongside my studies.

Beyond the physical aspect, these friendly matches are a great way to connect with others, develop team spirit, and release stress after intense academic periods.

Aeronautics and Gliding

I've always been deeply interested in aeronautics. During secondary school, I started training for a glider pilot's license. After around fifteen hours of flight, I got to learn firsthand how an aircraft behaves in the air and how much focus and anticipation flying really requires.

To understand the mechanics behind the flight, I also completed an observation internship at the Beynes airfield, where I assisted with glider maintenance.

These demanding but fascinating experiences strengthened my curiosity for technical systems and taught me to stay calm and attentive in challenging situations.
