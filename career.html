Professional Experiences

Observation Internship – Beynes Aerodrome

During my internship at Beynes aerodrome, I focused primarily on the technical maintenance of the glider fleet. I shadowed the mechanics in the workshop, observing how aircraft structures are inspected and prepared.

This hands-on experience gave me a concrete understanding of the mechanical rigor and strict safety protocols required in aviation maintenance.

Seasonal Employment – G20 Supermarket

June 2026 – August 2026

Over the summer of 2026, I worked as a seasonal employee at a G20 supermarket. This role required adaptability, customer service skills, and efficient inventory management in a fast-paced retail environment.

It was a valuable professional experience that taught me teamwork, responsibility, and the importance of maintaining high organizational standards under pressure.
